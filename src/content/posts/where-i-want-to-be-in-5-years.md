This is my first blog post. I have always wanted to start one, but just never have. My name is Mohamed Reda and I currently work as a Junior QA Support & DevOps Engineer. I recently graduated from Mohawk with an Advanced Diploma in Software Development. I will continue my education by obtaining my Bachelors in Software Engineering at McMaster University.

Now, for the ACTUAL blog, I'm not really sure where I want to be in 5 years. It is difficult to draw a roadmap right now in my life. Every week it feels like I switch where I want to focus my career on. Whether it be Embedded, AI Engineer, or even Quant. I love math, and low-level engineering so possibly either of those would work.

There are also days where I want to open a restaurant. I love cooking and it's a passion of mine. I recently smoked a chuck roast. I smoked it for 4-5 hours at 275-300 degrees until it reached about 170. I then braised it in beef stock until it reached 205, where it became extremely tender. While it cooled down, I had marinated chicken drumsticks for about 24 hours, and smoked those while waiting for the chuck to cool down. Ended up having the best smoked barbecue chuck roast sandwich of all time. Kinder's Hickory Brown Sugar Sauce is elite. It was by far the most tender piece of meat I have ever had. I smoked it with cherry wood as well, and it was insanely good and flavourful.

I also love basketball. I usually play basketball downtown with a bunch of people who I have been playing with for about 4 years. I don't know these guys off the court, I barely know their names, but I have seen them and played with them for a long time. It's funny how you can "know" someone for that long, and yet if you saw them in public you probably wouldn't say hello. Maybe that is just me though. Anyways, I played yesterday and I was playing really well. I'm 6'4" and about 230 pounds so I'm a big guy relative to others on the court. The general expectation is that I should be grabbing rebounds, and guarding the big man on the other team. I don't mind it as I play well in that position. My day before basketball was not the best, feeling down and not the happiest, but after I played for 2 hours, my mood shifted. I was happier and felt accomplished in my life, something I rarely feel in my current job. 

Sometimes I wish that my parents put me in basketball class, and really believed that this could be a possibility for their child. There was the basic expectation among immigrant parents that you had to graduate in a STEM field to be successful due to risk-to-reward ratio. When I have kids, I will make sure that they have the support system and the belief that they can do anything (literally anything).

To close off, I'm not sure where I want to be in 5 years, and I don't think I should. I'm still figuring things out it seems.
